# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Ivan for AI Engineer, Software Engineer, and Full Stack Engineer roles. They need to understand his strongest work, individual contribution, technical range, and readiness for a role within a short portfolio review.

## Product Purpose

Present credible engineering work clearly enough to support interview decisions while making the portfolio itself evidence of Ivan's ability to design and build a creative, distinctive web experience.

Success means a recruiter can quickly identify Ivan's focus, inspect relevant projects, verify experience, open the resume, and contact him, while remembering the portfolio as unusually crafted.

## Positioning

Ivan combines AI automation, enterprise software delivery, and full-stack implementation. The portfolio demonstrates that combination through real operational projects and an interactive presentation built by Ivan himself.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from a CV, LinkedIn, GitHub, or a job application. They may review the site quickly on a work laptop or phone, compare several candidates, open only one or two projects, and need direct access to the resume and contact details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion.
- Must retain working project detail routes, resume access, contact options, SEO metadata, and responsive behavior.
- Project descriptions and impact claims must come from the existing portfolio data; do not invent metrics or client outcomes.
- Creative interaction must not hide essential recruiter information or require an undisclosed gesture to navigate.
- Expensive visual effects must have a usable fallback and a reduced-motion path.

## Brand Commitments

- Personal identity: Ivander Johana Pratama / Ivan.
- Voice: confident, technically credible, concise, and approachable.
- Active Theory is a binding interaction reference for cinematic motion, spatial composition, and making the website itself demonstrate craft. Its navigation ambiguity and accessibility limitations are anti-references.
- Existing portrait, project screenshots, resume, contact details, and real project content remain source material.

## Evidence on Hand

- Profile and contact data in `json/profile.json`.
- Education, experience, achievements, and skills in `json/resume.json`.
- Thirteen project records with roles, summaries, technologies, impact statements, highlights, and available screenshots in `json/data.json` and `public/image/`.
- ATS resume at `public/docs/CV_IVANDERJP_ATS.pdf`.
- Public GitHub and LinkedIn profiles.
- No testimonials or additional performance metrics may be assumed.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work before listing the tools.
2. Make creativity reinforce technical credibility.
3. Give recruiters orientation within seconds.
4. Use motion to reveal relationships and progress, not to delay access.
5. Keep every claim traceable to real work or supplied evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, browser zoom, useful alternative text, responsive layouts, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Core content and navigation must remain available without advanced motion or WebGL.
